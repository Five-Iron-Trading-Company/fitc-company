About us

A small firm with a long memory.

We started in 2013 with a simple rule: know your risk before you take it. We still run that way.

Our approach

Five Iron Trading Company trades its own capital. We do not manage outside money, which keeps our interests plain: we do well when our discipline does.

Every position has an owner, a limit and a daily explanation. Risk reports to leadership, not to the desks, and settlements are reconciled the same day.

Milestones

2013
Firm founded in New York with a single trading desk.
2016
Risk and trade operations established as independent functions.
2019
In-house trading and risk platform replaces spreadsheets and vendor tools.
2022
London team opens to cover settlements and the early session.
Today
Four desks and eleven people on one platform.
