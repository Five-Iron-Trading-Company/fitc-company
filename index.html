Our mission

To trade with discipline, so that risk is always understood before it is taken.

Five Iron Trading Company trades its own capital across cash and derivative markets. We exist to earn steady, durable returns by combining careful research, firm risk limits and clean operations — and by being straightforward about all three.

What we are working toward

Four goals guide how we allocate capital, time and attention.

Durable returns

We measure ourselves over years, not quarters. Survival and consistency come before size.

Risk before return

Every position has a limit that is set before the trade, reviewed independently and enforced without exception.

Operational excellence

Trades are confirmed, settled and reconciled the same day. Breaks are found by us, not by our counterparties.

Technology we own

We build and run our own trading and risk platform, so the tools fit the work and the people understand them.

What we value

Accountability

Every position, process and system has a named owner who can explain it.

Independence

Risk and operations report separately from the desks they review.

Integrity

We deal plainly with counterparties, regulators and each other.

Craft

We prefer fewer things done carefully over many things done quickly.

Where we operate

Offices in New York and London, covering the full trading day from the early European session to the US close.
